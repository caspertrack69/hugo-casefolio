Casefolio is a reusable Hugo theme for developers, studios, and technical writers who want to publish project case studies and technical articles from a single static site.

This page is a standard Hugo content page rendered with the theme's default single layout. Edit it freely — nothing about the site owner is hard-coded in the templates.

## How this theme is organized

- **Projects** are case studies authored as Markdown page bundles under `content/projects/`.
- **Writing** is your technical blog, authored under `content/posts/`.
- **About** is this page.
- Identity, navigation, hero copy, sections, and footer are all driven by `hugo.toml`.

## Contact

Reach out through the links in the footer. The example site uses neutral, clearly-fictitious demo contact details — replace them with your own.
